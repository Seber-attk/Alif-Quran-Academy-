Privacy Policy

Sira Quran Academy · Last updated: October 23, 2026

Sira Quran Academy helps people learn to read Arabic letters and the Quran, including live one-to-one recitation sessions with a teacher. This policy explains what we collect and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it.
  • Learning data: lessons completed, quiz results, answer times, star ratings, XP, streaks and badges.
  • Live session data: the Quran page you are on and the words your teacher marks during a session.
  • Microphone: used only during live audio sessions, with your permission. We do not record calls.
  • On your device: sound and reminder preferences are saved in your browser/app storage.
  • Live class payment verification: if you pay for a live class by bank transfer, you submit the bank reference number, the amount, and optionally the sender's name, so our admin can confirm the payment against the bank account. We do not collect your card number, bank password, or any other banking credentials — payment itself happens directly with your bank, outside the app.
We do not collect your location, contacts, or photos, and we show no ads.

2. How we use it

To run your account, save and show your progress, connect you with a teacher for live sessions, verify live class payments, send reminders you turned on, and reply to support requests. We do not sell your data.

3. Services that process data for us

  • Google Firebase (login, database, hosting)
  • EmailJS (sending notification emails)
  • Jitsi Meet (live audio calls)
  • Al Quran Cloud (Quran text)
  • jsDelivr (code libraries)

Each of these has its own privacy policy.

4. Who can see your data

You, your assigned teacher (your progress and live session marks), and the app administrator (including payment verification details, to confirm and approve live class payments). Nobody else.

5. Who this app is for

Sira Quran Academy is intended for adult learners and teenagers aged 13 and over, including new Muslims learning to read the Quran. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. To delete your account and all its data:
  • In the app: Account → Delete my account, or
  • Email quranacademyalif@gmail.com from your registered email with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days.
Payment verification records (bank reference numbers) are kept for a reasonable period for bookkeeping and dispute resolution, even after other data is deleted, unless you specifically ask us to remove them too.

7. Security

Data is stored with Google Firebase over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to what each role needs.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: quranacademyalif@gmail.com
